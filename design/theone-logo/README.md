## 应用图标最终提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: final square desktop application icon, one standalone image, 1024x1024 or higher.
Primary request: Create an original, exceptionally simple, memorable symbol for TheOne, a local desktop AI work application that brings conversation, project files and saved work into one place.
Concept: a bold sculptural numeral 1 made from two interlocking flat geometric strokes, conveying several capabilities gathering into one clear action. The silhouette should read as a distinctive upward 1, not an arrow, and be visually balanced. A diagonal cut or open seam can give a subtle sense of convergence. Use very few shapes, generous thickness, no fine detail; immediately readable at 32px.
Scene/backdrop: a deep midnight navy rounded-square app tile filling most of a square image, with genuinely transparent outside corners. Large ivory central mark and a restrained vivid jade green secondary plane or stroke, integrated as part of the numeral rather than decorative floating pieces.
Style: precise flat vector-like graphic design, strong silhouette, clean edges, premium calm desktop software identity. No three-dimensional extrusion, no gradients, no texture, no shadows. Centered, optical balance, ample breathing room.
Text: none.
Avoid: stars, sparkles, generic AI starbursts, robots, brains, circuitry, pi symbol, existing brand marks, chain links, infinity loops, decorative mockup scenes, watermarks. Do not imitate Mu, AionUI or OpenAI.
Output only the finished app icon.
```

Create one beautifully restrained landscape brand image on a warm off-white background. Place the reference app tile at the left, at moderate scale, and the word TheOne to its right, as a single centered horizontal brand signature with generous surrounding whitespace. Flat graphic presentation, crisp edges. No mockup devices, no perspective, no ornamental graphics, no captions, no subtitle, no drop shadows. Keep the mark faithful to the reference, no new symbols, no star, no pi. Output one finished landscape image.
